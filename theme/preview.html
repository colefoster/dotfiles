Switch with theme-set <name>. It rewrites one word in theme/active, pushes the gap numbers into the marked block in aerospace.toml, then reloads SketchyBar, JankyBorders and the AeroSpace config in place. AeroSpace is never restarted, so windows keep their places — the gaps just re-flow around them.
