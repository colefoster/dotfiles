Switch with mull theme <name> and mull layout <name>. Each rewrites one word under mullion/, pushes the gap numbers into the marked block in aerospace.toml, then reloads SketchyBar, JankyBorders and the AeroSpace config in place. AeroSpace is never restarted, so windows keep their places — the gaps just re-flow around them. A bottom bar moves the reserved strip to the foot of the screen and hands the notch back to macOS.
